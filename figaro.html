Figaro Branding

Figaro Coffee is a premium coffee brand that blends quality, culture, and community. Established to provide a uniquely Filipino coffee experience, Figaro Coffee offers a range of specialty beverages, freshly roasted beans, and artisanal pastries, all crafted to deliver a warm and inviting café experience.

Graphic Design

Developing the Concept

The core idea behind the branding was wholeness, bringing together coffee, culture, and the Filipino identity into a single visual system. I translated this concept through the logo, patterns, and color choices, drawing inspiration from local motifs while keeping the overall look modern and premium. The goal was to create a design that feels rooted in tradition but still relevant in a contemporary café setting.

Applying the Design Across Materials

I focused on making sure the identity stayed consistent across different brand applications, from print materials to packaging. I explored multiple image variations to test how the visuals adapt in different contexts while maintaining cohesion. Each application was designed to reinforce brand recognition and reflect Figaro’s premium yet approachable personality.

Designing the Brand and the Packaging

Working on the Figaro Coffee branding allowed me to balance strategy with creativity. Unlike my previous projects, this one required honoring tradition while incorporating modern elements, creating a brand identity that felt both timeless and contemporary. I had to carefully consider the brand’s identity, target audience, and market positioning while translating these insights into visual elements that were engaging and memorable.
