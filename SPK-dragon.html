Soldier, Poet, King Design & Animation

A fantasy-adventure film that brings storytelling to life through a rich blend of 2D and 3D animation. Created by multimedia arts students, the film explores imagination, identity, and transformation through a powerful visual and emotional journey.

Graphic Design

3d & Animation

The Film Concept and Graphic Design

The film used attention-grabbing visuals from its official poster to animation, the visual direction leans on heavy editing and complex and stimulating images that balances the plot and aesthetics. In the official poster, emphasis was placed on the characters, setting, and overall atmosphere to evoke curiosity and anticipation.

Animation

The animation of the Soldier, Poet, King, and Dragon, along with the poster design, required a lot of work using After Effects, Premiere, and CapCut. The animations were very time-consuming, but they were handled well, balancing both creativity and practicality. In the 3D animation, the primary objective was to achieve harmony between visual effects and storytelling, ensuring that the film’s artistic elements enhanced, rather than overshadowed, its emotional and narrative core. Each character demanded careful attention to movement, expression, and style, which allowed me to experiment with creative ideas while keeping the work manageable.

Reflecting on the Design and Animation

Working on the animation of the Soldier, Poet, King, and Dragon was challenging but fulfilling. Seeing everything come together made the effort feel worth it. The process pushed my patience and focus, but it also allowed me to enjoy the creative side of experimenting with motion and visuals.
