Designing the StewardTrack Website

When designing the StewardTrack website, I focused on keeping things simple and easy to use, because the target audience needed a platform that’s straightforward and clear. I prioritized a clean layout, intuitive navigation, and readable typography, making sure that users can access important features without confusion. The design balances functionality with a friendly, approachable look, so that managing tasks and events feels effortless. My goal was to create a website that’s not just visually appealing but also genuinely useful for the people who rely on it every day.
