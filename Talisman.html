Talisman: Streetwear Design

Talisman is a clothing shop inspired by Filipino culture, mythology, and folklore. The brand incorporates traditional symbols, stories, and local influences into contemporary clothing, blending heritage with modern design.

Designing the Talisman Shirt

Developing Talisman was a rewarding experience that pushed me to grow creatively. Although working through the details, especially those rooted in Filipino culture and mythology, required a lot of time and focus, the process helped me better understand the meaning behind each element. Refining these details allowed me to create designs that felt thoughtful and intentional.
