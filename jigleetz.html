About the Company

What began as door-to-door sales evolved into an online sensation and now thrives as a local sweets shop, committed to delivering fun, flavor, and nostalgia with a creative twist. With a passion for crafting unique and playful treats, On Sweets! blends traditional candy-making with imaginative flavor combinations and eye-catching designs. Each product is made with care, reflecting the brand’s mission to spark happiness and create memorable experiences for all ages. Whether you're satisfying a sweet craving or sharing a gift, On Sweets! makes every moment a little sweeter.

Social Media Posts

For Jigleetz’ social media, the design presents a fun and colorful personality that grabs attention and reflects the playful spirit of the brand. I used bright visuals, bold graphics, and energetic layouts to make each post stand out, while staying on trend with popular styles like the popular movie, Wicked, and other vibrant, snack-focused content. Playful messaging and creative imagery made the posts feel lively and perfectly aligned with the brand’s sweet, cheerful identity.

Designing the Brand and the Packaging

Jigleets by On Sweets! come in a colorful lineup bursting with personality, from the smooth, nostalgic sweetness of the Original, to the tangy, lively kick of Berry, the fresh and trendy vibe of Matcha’s earthy green, and the playful spookiness of Halloween with its bold orange and black hues. Each flavor delivers a unique, joyful experience wrapped in vibrant colors that make every bite an exciting adventure.
